Privacy policy

Controller: Stefano Toncelli · Contact: ste.toncelli@gmail.com
Last updated: 28 September 2026

LifeNex is an iOS app that helps you observe how the data already in Apple Health evolves and stay motivated toward goals you choose. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose, and does not prescribe intensity or therapy.

Data the app reads

With your consent, LifeNex reads from Health (HealthKit), read-only:

  • activity (steps, energy, exercise minutes, distance)
  • workouts (sessions, GPS route if present, effort, session heart rate)
  • cardio (HR, resting HR, HRV, VO₂ max)
  • body (weight, height, BMI, body fat and lean mass)
  • sleep and, if present in Health, vitals such as blood pressure and oxygen saturation
  • running and walking dynamics, if written by your device

LifeNex does not write or modify Health data.

Where data is processed

Health data stays on your iPhone (local store). There is no mandatory LifeNex account.

Optional smart analysis: only if you enable consent in Settings and you request a reading (Generate / Update), LifeNex may use numeric summaries already computed in the app (averages, counts, comparisons) to prepare text via a secure gateway. GPS tracks, photos and raw Health history are not sent. You can revoke consent at any time.

What we do not do

  • we do not sell your data
  • we do not use Health data for advertising or cross-app tracking
  • we do not share health data with third parties for commercial purposes

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the device while the app is installed. To delete it: remove LifeNex from your iPhone and, in Settings → Health → Apps and Data Access, revoke access for LifeNex.

Permissions

You can refuse or revoke Health permissions at any time. Without permissions the app works in a limited or empty way.

Children

LifeNex is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Subscriptions

Plus and Max subscriptions are managed by Apple through the App Store. LifeNex reads subscription status to unlock features and smart-reading quotas. Billing follows Apple’s rules.

Changes

If some data ever leaves the phone in the future (only with explicit consent), this page will be updated first.
